Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Who is responsible for Sortline?

Sortline is developed and operated by Riush ("Sortline", "we", "us" or "our").

Privacy and support contact: sortline.contact@gmail.com.

2. What data may be processed?

Sortline is designed to collect only the information needed to provide game features, online services, purchases, advertising choices and technical operation. The exact data processed depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the version of the game installed on your device.

Feature Data that may be processed Purpose
Account / authentication Sortline player identifier, authentication/session information and linked platform identifiers where applicable. Create or access your Sortline player identity and keep online features associated with the correct player.
Google Play Games Google Play Games player identifier and profile information made available to Sortline after sign-in, such as a display name, where applicable. Platform sign-in and Play Games features.
Leaderboards Player identifier, display name if used, scores and leaderboard-related game data. Show rankings and compare scores.
Cloud Save Game progress, settings or other gameplay values selected for cloud storage. Restore or synchronize progress across sessions or devices.
Purchases Purchase status, product identifiers, transaction or receipt information needed to validate and restore purchases. Process, validate and restore in-app purchases. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not directly by Sortline.
Advertising and consent Advertising, consent, device and technical information may be processed by Google services depending on your region, device settings, consent choices and ad configuration. Show ads, apply consent choices, limit or personalize advertising where permitted, and prevent abuse.
Analytics — only if enabled Game events, session information, device/technical information and performance data configured in the analytics service. Understand how the game is used, improve balancing and identify technical issues.

3. Why do we use this information?

  • Provide and maintain Sortline and its online features.
  • Authenticate players and associate progress with the correct player identity.
  • Provide leaderboards and other competitive features.
  • Save and restore game progress through Unity Cloud Save.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases.
  • Show advertising and apply consent choices where advertising is enabled.
  • Detect abuse, cheating, fraud or technical problems.
  • Improve the game using aggregated or gameplay information where analytics is enabled.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests.

4. Third-party services

Sortline may use third-party platform and infrastructure providers. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy terms and by the configuration used in Sortline.

  • Google Play Games — platform identity and Play Games features.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, where ads are enabled.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — consent messaging and consent choices, where applicable.
  • Google Play Billing — processing of in-app purchases.
  • Unity Authentication — player authentication and app-specific player identity.
  • Unity Leaderboards — leaderboard scores and related player information.
  • Unity Cloud Save — storage of game progress and related gameplay data associated with the player.
  • Analytics provider — only if analytics is enabled in the released build.

5. Advertising

If advertising is enabled, Sortline may use Google AdMob and Google UMP. Depending on your location, device settings, age-related settings, consent choices and Google’s configuration, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized. Sortline should request and respect consent where required by the applicable platform or law.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play. Sortline may receive information needed to confirm that a purchase was completed, restore an entitlement or provide purchased content. Sortline does not directly receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.

7. Data retention

Account-linked data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain account integrity, resolve support issues, prevent abuse or meet applicable legal obligations. Data stored by third-party providers may also be subject to their retention rules and the configuration used by Sortline.

When a valid account-deletion request is completed, the Sortline account and associated user data within the scope of the request are deleted. Where service providers process account-linked data for Sortline, we will also request deletion through the controls those providers make available. Limited information may be retained only where necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction-record purposes.

8. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion from within the game through Profile → Manage Account → Delete Account.

If you no longer have access to the game, use our external deletion page: Sortline Account Deletion.

Account deletion may include cloud-saved gameplay data associated with the Sortline player account.

Deleting your Sortline account does not delete your Google account or your general Google Play Games profile.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, such as asking for access, correction or deletion, or objecting to or restricting certain processing. You can contact us at sortline.contact@gmail.com.

Advertising and consent choices may also be available through the consent interface shown in the game and through your device or Google account settings.

10. Audience

Sortline is not specifically designed as a child-directed service. If Sortline is later distributed to audiences or age groups that require child-specific privacy treatment, the game's SDK configuration, store declarations and this policy must be reviewed before that release.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a small online game. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sortline, its online services or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, support or account deletion: sortline.contact@gmail.com.
