Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who is responsible for Sortline?

Sortline is developed and operated by Riush ("Sortline", "we", "us" or "our").

Privacy and support contact: sortline.contact@gmail.com.

2. What data may be processed?

Sortline is designed to collect only the information needed to provide game features, online services, purchases, advertising choices and technical operation. The exact data processed depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the version of the game installed on your device.

Feature Data that may be processed Purpose
Account / authentication Sortline player identifier, authentication/session information and linked platform identifiers where applicable. Create or access your Sortline player identity and keep online features associated with the correct player.
Google Play Games Google Play Games player identifier and profile information made available to Sortline after sign-in, such as a display name, where applicable. Platform sign-in and Play Games features.
Leaderboards Player identifier, display name if used, scores and leaderboard-related game data. Show rankings and compare scores.
Cloud Save Game progress, settings or other gameplay values selected for cloud storage. Restore or synchronize progress across sessions or devices.
Purchases Purchase status, product identifiers, transaction or receipt information needed to validate and restore purchases. Process, validate and restore in-app purchases. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not directly by Sortline.
Advertising and consent Advertising, consent, device and technical information may be processed by Google services depending on your region, device settings, consent choices and ad configuration. Show ads, apply consent choices, limit or personalize advertising where permitted, and prevent abuse.
GameAnalytics — disabled in the commercial Android release That release does not initialize GameAnalytics or send new gameplay, session or purchase statistics to it. Data from an earlier installation may still exist with the provider. Support for requests concerning historical data.

3. Why do we use this information?

  • Provide and maintain Sortline and its online features.
  • Authenticate players and associate progress with the correct player identity.
  • Provide leaderboards and other competitive features.
  • Save and restore game progress through Unity Cloud Save.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases.
  • Show advertising and apply consent choices where advertising is enabled.
  • Detect abuse, cheating, fraud or technical problems.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests.

4. Third-party services

Sortline may use third-party platform and infrastructure providers. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy terms and by the configuration used in Sortline.

  • Google Play Games — platform identity and Play Games features.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, where ads are enabled.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — consent messaging and consent choices, where applicable.
  • Google Play Billing — processing of in-app purchases.
  • Unity Authentication — player authentication and app-specific player identity.
  • Unity Leaderboards — leaderboard scores and related player information.
  • Unity Cloud Save — storage of game progress and related gameplay data associated with the player.
  • Unity Cloud Code and Google Cloud — server-side purchase validation, delivery records, recovery, fraud prevention and account-erasure maintenance.
  • GameAnalytics — collection is disabled in the commercial Android release. It cannot be enabled in that release through a player preference. Historical data from earlier installations is handled separately from the Sortline account.

5. Advertising

If advertising is enabled, Sortline may use Google AdMob and Google UMP. Depending on your location, device settings, age-related settings, consent choices and Google’s configuration, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized. Sortline should request and respect consent where required by the applicable platform or law.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play. Unity server services and Google Cloud infrastructure validate purchases, maintain delivery and restoration records, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims. The records can include the Sortline player identifier, purchase tokens, product identifiers, purchase status and delivery information. Google Play handles payment-card details; Sortline does not receive your full payment-card details.

Purchase records are associated with the recoverable Sortline account. Deleting that account removes its account-linked purchase and delivery records through the account-erasure process. It does not delete your Google account or Google's purchase history, or itself issue a refund. Contact support before deleting an account if you have a purchase issue.

7. Data retention

While an account or its registered purchase rights remain active, we keep the minimum purchase records needed to deliver and restore those rights, prevent duplicate claims and handle purchase issues. There is no general 30-day expiry for active purchase rights. Other account-linked progress and settings are kept for the relevant feature until erased.

When account erasure begins, identifiable purchase and delivery records are removed through that process. Minimum pseudonymous purchase hashes and scope/date metadata may remain for fraud prevention for up to 30 days from the start of erasure. These hashes are not treated as anonymous data. Retries do not restart the period. Cleanup is scheduled to begin on day 29.

A temporary technical case retains the Unity identifier and references needed to block old sessions and verify completion. After confirming that the authentication identity is absent, we wait 65 minutes for old sessions to expire and target removal of that case in the next maintenance run, with a maximum operational deadline of 24 hours from that confirmation. If erasure or maintenance fails, we investigate it and do not report incomplete erasure as complete.

For a refund notice that cannot be linked to any account, only a minimum purchase hash and scope/date metadata may be kept for up to 30 days from Google's refund-event date. Repeated notices do not renew that period. An older purchase without a verifiable server record may require support review; existing registered rights do not expire on that basis.

GameAnalytics history is independent of the Sortline / Unity account. Disabling collection, deleting the Sortline account or forgetting a local identifier does not itself erase that provider's history. For a historical-data request, we aim to forward the minimum necessary game and SDK user identifiers to the provider within 24 hours and keep the minimum support case until resolution, for at most 30 days from the request. We escalate unresolved cases before that limit. This is our support-case period, not a claim about the provider's retention or deletion-completion time.

Google Play, advertising services and other providers also process information under their own terms and retention rules. The Sortline purchase-erasure process does not automatically erase Google's transaction history, a Google account or independent advertising/analytics data.

8. Account and data deletion

Request account erasure through Profile → Manage Account → Delete Account. This covers the Sortline / Unity identity, cloud progress and recovery copies, leaderboard data and account-linked purchase records within the scope described above.

If you no longer have the game, use Sortline Account Deletion or contact sortline.contact@gmail.com. We verify ownership before removing account data.

For historical GameAnalytics data, open the usage-statistics settings and its GameAnalytics data panel to copy the locally retained SDK identifier or prepare an email to support. You must send the draft yourself. We do not link that local reference to your Unity identifier. If the reference is unavailable, contact us; we cannot promise to reconstruct an identifier from an older or uninstalled version. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Deleting Sortline data does not delete your Google account, general Google Play Games profile or Google purchase history. See the separate retention and provider-history limits above.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, such as asking for access, correction or deletion, or objecting to or restricting certain processing. You can contact us at sortline.contact@gmail.com.

Advertising and consent choices may also be available through the consent interface shown in the game and through your device or Google account settings.

10. Audience

Sortline is not specifically designed as a child-directed service. If Sortline is later distributed to audiences or age groups that require child-specific privacy treatment, the game's SDK configuration, store declarations and this policy must be reviewed before that release.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a small online game. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sortline, its online services or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

13. Contact
