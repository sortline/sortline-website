Account & data deletion

Delete your Sortline account

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What this deletes

This process requests deletion of your Sortline account and the user data associated with that account that Sortline controls or stores through its service providers.

Deleting your Sortline account does not delete your Google account and does not delete your general Google Play Games profile.

Option 1 — Delete from inside Sortline

If you still have access to the game, use:

Profile → Manage Account → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps shown in the game. This is the preferred route because the game can identify the correct Sortline player account directly.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the game

If you have uninstalled Sortline or cannot access the in-game deletion option, email us using the button below.

Request account deletion

Use the subject Sortline Account Deletion Request and include:

  • your Sortline Player ID, if you still have it;
  • your in-game or Google Play Games display name, if applicable;
  • a short statement confirming that you want the Sortline account deleted;
  • any additional information we reasonably request to verify that the account belongs to you.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

How to find your Player ID

If you still have access to Sortline, open the profile/account area and use the Player ID shown there. If the released version provides a copy button, use it to avoid typing mistakes.

If you cannot access the Player ID, you can still contact us. We may ask for limited additional information needed to locate and verify the correct Sortline account.

Data included in the deletion request

Depending on which Sortline features you used, deletion may include:

  • the Sortline / Unity player identity associated with the account;
  • leaderboard entries and account-linked leaderboard data associated with that player;
  • game progress, settings and other gameplay data stored through Unity Cloud Save;
  • other account-linked settings or progress stored online;
  • other account-linked user data held by Sortline or its processors within the scope of the request.

Some limited records may be retained where necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction-record obligations. Any such retention should be limited to what is necessary.

Processing time

We aim to confirm and complete valid deletion requests without undue delay and normally within 30 days. If we need additional information to identify or verify the account, we will contact you.

Need general support instead?

For bugs, purchase issues or other questions that are not account-deletion requests, use the Sortline Support page.
