Account & data deletion

Delete your Sortline account

Last updated: October 24, 2026

What this deletes

This process requests deletion of your Sortline account and the user data associated with that account that Sortline controls or stores through its service providers.

Deleting your Sortline account does not delete your Google account and does not delete your general Google Play Games profile.

Option 1 — Delete from inside Sortline

If you still have access to the game, use:

Profile → Manage Account → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps shown in the game. This is the preferred route because the game can identify the correct Sortline player account directly.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the game

If you have uninstalled Sortline or cannot access the in-game deletion option, email us using the button below.

Request account deletion

Use the subject Sortline Account Deletion Request and include:

  • your Sortline Player ID, if you still have it;
  • your in-game or Google Play Games display name, if applicable;
  • a short statement confirming that you want the Sortline account deleted;
  • any additional information we reasonably request to verify that the account belongs to you.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

How to find your Player ID

If you still have access to Sortline, open the profile/account area and use the Player ID shown there. If the released version provides a copy button, use it to avoid typing mistakes.

If you cannot access the Player ID, you can still contact us. We may ask for limited additional information needed to locate and verify the correct Sortline account.

Data included in the deletion request

Depending on the features you used, the Sortline account-erasure process includes:

  • the Sortline / Unity player identity;
  • leaderboard entries and account-linked ranking data;
  • cloud progress, settings and recovery copies in the Sortline service environments;
  • account-linked purchase, validation and delivery records.

It does not delete your Google account, general Google Play Games profile or Google's purchase history, and does not itself issue a refund. Deleting the account can remove records used to restore content, so contact support first if you have a purchase issue.

Minimum pseudonymous purchase hashes and scope/date metadata may remain for up to 30 days from the start of erasure, without extending that period on retries. A temporary technical case retains the Unity identifier and references needed to block old sessions and verify completion. After confirmed authentication removal, we wait 65 minutes and target removal in the next maintenance run, with a maximum operational deadline of 24 hours from that confirmation. Incomplete operations are investigated rather than reported as complete.

Refund notices without an identifiable account have a separate maximum 30-day period from Google's event date. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Processing time

We aim to confirm and complete valid deletion requests without undue delay and normally within 30 days. We may request limited information to identify and verify your account. This request-handling target does not restart the technical retention periods above.

Historical GameAnalytics data

Collection is disabled in the commercial Android release. Data from earlier installations may still exist with GameAnalytics and requires a separate request. In the game, the usage-statistics settings and GameAnalytics data panel can copy a locally retained SDK identifier or prepare an email to support. You must send the draft yourself. Forgetting the local reference or deleting the Sortline account does not erase provider history.

Contact sortline.contact@gmail.com and specify that your request concerns GameAnalytics. If available, include the SDK user identifier; the game is Sortline (GameAnalytics game 353462). If the identifier is unavailable, ask support what can be located. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

We aim to forward a verified historical-data request to GameAnalytics within 24 hours and keep a minimum support case until resolution, for at most 30 days from the request. We escalate unresolved cases before that limit. These are Sortline's support targets; they do not specify the provider's retention or deletion-completion time.

Need general support instead?

For bugs, purchase issues or other questions that are not account-deletion requests, use the Sortline Support page.
