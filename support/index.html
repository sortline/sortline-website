General support

For technical problems, purchase issues, account access problems or other questions, contact: sortline.contact@gmail.com.

Email Sortline Support

What to include

To help us investigate quickly, include as much of the following as you can:

  • Player ID, if available;
  • Sortline version;
  • device model and Android version;
  • a clear description of the problem;
  • steps that reproduce the problem, if known;
  • a screenshot when it is useful and does not contain sensitive information.

Never send passwords, full payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Account deletion is handled separately

If your request is specifically to delete your Sortline account and associated data, use the dedicated Account Deletion page. This keeps deletion requests clear and easy to identify.
